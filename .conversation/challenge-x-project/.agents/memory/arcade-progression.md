---
name: Desafio Infinito: progress and rewards
description: Product boundaries for local saves, personal seasons, and virtual currency.
---

For Desafio Infinito, preserve each player's existing local progress when changing progression or storage. Ranking is a personal, on-device 30-day season, not an online leaderboard. Fichas are virtual and have no cash value; Android coin-package messaging is informational and must not imply that a payment was processed.

**Why:** The user explicitly asked to preserve saved data, keep ranking local, and avoid fake payments.

**How to apply:** Keep these boundaries explicit in future work on saves, seasons, rewards, or the shop. Do not reset or replace stored progress as a shortcut.
